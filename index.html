How to use HumanBody 3D

  • Drag to rotate · Scroll to zoom · Right-drag to pan · Click a part to study it.
  • Pick a level at the top: the same 3D body is explained differently for Class 1, Class 6, Class 10, an undergraduate or a PhD student.
  • Toggle systems on the left to add or remove layers — skin, bone, muscle, organs, vessels, nerves.
  • Use X-ray to fade the skin, Slice to cut the body open, and Micro view (inside a part, at Undergraduate level or above) to zoom into cells and tissues.
  • Press 🎯 Quiz to test yourself and 🎒 Tour for a guided lesson at your level.
  • Shortcuts: 1–5 level · / search · X x-ray · R reset view · Esc close panels.

This atlas is an educational model. Anatomy is stylised, not patient-specific, and it is not a medical device: always consult a clinician for medical decisions.
